Beyond RPA: Truly Agentic Automation

Traditional RPA (Robotic Process Automation) breaks when pages change. Rule-based workflows break when edge cases appear. Agentic automation is different — AI agents can read, reason, and adapt.

An agentic workflow agent doesn't just click buttons. It understands context, handles exceptions, asks clarifying questions when needed, and learns from outcomes over time.

Bytolix builds agentic automation on n8n, Python, and custom agent frameworks — integrating with Slack, Jira, Salesforce, SAP, and any API your business runs on.
