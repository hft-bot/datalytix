AI Governance & Agent Observability

Permissions maps, escalation logic, audit trails, and continuous evaluation — so your AI agents run safely in production under real compliance constraints, including the EU AI Act.

Agents in Production Need Governance

An AI agent that works in a demo is very different from one that's safe, reliable, and compliant in production. Without proper governance, agents hallucinate, exceed their permissions, make costly errors, and create legal exposure.

Bytolix builds governance and observability frameworks into every production deployment — not as an afterthought, but as a core part of the architecture.

Full compliance support for EU AI Act, SOC 2, and industry-specific regulatory requirements.

Governance Framework Components

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓
